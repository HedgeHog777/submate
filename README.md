# SubMate

A simple Android application for managing subscriptions and recurring payments.

---

## The Idea

People often forget how much money they spend on subscriptions every month.

SubMate helps users keep track of recurring payments in one place and clearly shows future expenses.

---

## Goal

Make subscription management as simple as checking today's weather.

Open → See → Close.

No complicated budgeting.

No accounting.

Just subscriptions.

---

## MVP

- Add subscription
- Categories
- Billing cycle
- Next payment date
- Monthly total
- Yearly total
- Notifications

---

## Planned Features

- Statistics
- Charts
- Multiple currencies
- Export / Import
- Backup
- Widgets

---

## What it is NOT

❌ Bank

❌ Expense tracker

❌ Accounting software

❌ Personal finance manager

SubMate focuses on subscriptions and recurring payments only.

---

## Technologies

- Kotlin
- Jetpack Compose
- Room Database
- Material 3

---

## Status

🟡 Planning

---

## Why?

The idea came from personal experience.

Managing subscriptions across different services quickly becomes confusing.

SubMate aims to solve exactly this problem while staying lightweight and offline-first.
